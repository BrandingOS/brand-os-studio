23 / 32Imagery

Brand matter.

Images are monochrome, grained and halftoned — matter, not stock. They show material, depth and movement, never people pretending to work.

Use halftone, grain, macro texture, soft volume, deep blacks.

Avoid colour photography, stock scenes, gradients with hue, glossy 3D.
