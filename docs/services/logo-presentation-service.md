## PHASE 2 — IMPROVED PRESENTATION LOGIC

### The Premium Logo Presentation Framework

**Core principle:** Every slide must either **build anticipation**, **reveal the concept**, **prove the logic**, or **make it real**.

### Master Slide Sequence (per concept)

| Slide | Name | Duration | Purpose |
|-------|------|----------|---------|
| 1 | **Concept Title** | 3 sec | Name the direction. Set the mood. One line. |
| 2 | **The Idea** | 10 sec | 1-2 sentences explaining the core concept. What's the thinking? |
| 3 | **Logo Reveal** | 5 sec | Full-size logo on clean background. Let it breathe. |
| 4 | **Construction** | 8 sec | Grid, geometry, or symbol breakdown. Show the precision. |
| 5 | **Why It Works** | 8 sec | 3-4 bullet points connecting the design to the brand brief. |
| 6 | **Variations** | 5 sec | Primary, mark, wordmark, stacked. Clean grid. |
| 7 | **Color & Mono** | 5 sec | On white, on black, on brand color, monochrome. |
| 8 | **In Context** | 8 sec | 2-3 application mockups. Make it real. |

**Total per concept: 8 slides, ~52 seconds of attention**

### Full Deck Structure

```
SECTION 1: SETUP (4 slides)
├── Cover (brand name + "Logo Concepts")
├── The Brief (what the client asked for — mirrors their words back)
├── Our Approach (1 slide, not 5 — visual, not text-heavy)
└── Brand Personality (3-5 attributes that guided the design)

SECTION 2: CONCEPT A (8 slides)
├── Concept Title ("The Mark of Precision")
├── The Idea (1-2 sentences)
├── Logo Reveal (hero moment)
├── Construction (grid/geometry)
├── Why It Works (3-4 points)
├── Variations (primary, mark, wordmark)
├── Color & Mono (4 treatments)
└── In Context (2-3 mockups)

SECTION 3: CONCEPT B (8 slides — same structure)

SECTION 4: CONCEPT C (8 slides — same structure)

SECTION 5: DECISION (3 slides)
├── Side by Side (all 3 concepts compared)
├── Recommendation (which one we'd choose and why)
└── Next Steps (what happens after they decide)

SECTION 6: CLOSE (1 slide)
└── Thank You + Contact
```

**Total: 28 slides for 3 concepts**

## PHASE 3 — SLIDE-BY-SLIDE CONTENT GUIDE

### SETUP SLIDES

**Slide 1: Cover**
```
[Brand Name]
Logo Concepts

Prepared for [Client Name]
[Date]

[Your studio logo, small, bottom]
```
- Dark background, large typography, clean
- No contact details on cover (save for end)

**Slide 2: The Brief**
```
WHAT YOU TOLD US

"[Quote from the client brief — their own words]"

Industry: [X]
Audience: [X]
Feel: [X]
```
- Shows you listened. Mirrors their language.
- Builds trust before showing work.

**Slide 3: Our Approach**
```
[Visual timeline or 3 icons]
Research → Explore → Refine

"We explored [X] directions and refined to 3 distinct concepts."
```
- ONE slide, not five. Visual, not text.

**Slide 4: Brand Personality**
```
[3-5 words arranged large]

Bold. Precise. Human.

"These attributes guided every design decision."
```
- Sets the filter for evaluating the logos.

### PER-CONCEPT SLIDES

**Concept Title Slide**
```
CONCEPT A

"The Anchor"

[Subtitle: one line describing the direction]
```
- Full-bleed color or dark bg
- Large type, minimal

**The Idea**
```
[Concept name]

"The mark combines [X] and [Y] to represent [Z].
It communicates [brand attribute] through [design element]."
```
- MAX 2 sentences. If you need more, the concept isn't clear enough.

**Logo Reveal**
```
[Logo centered, generous white space]
[Nothing else on the slide]
```
- This is the hero moment. No distractions.

**Construction**
```
[Grid overlay showing geometry]
[Or: elements that compose the mark, deconstructed]
```
- Shows intelligence and intention behind the form.

**Why It Works**
```
✓ Connects to [brand value]
✓ Distinctive in [industry]  
✓ Scalable from favicon to billboard
✓ [Specific brand-brief alignment point]
```
- Max 4 points. Each must be specific, not generic.

**Variations**
```
[4-up grid]
Primary Logo | Logomark | Wordmark | Stacked
```
- Clean, organized, labeled.

**Color & Mono**
```
[4-up grid]
On White | On Dark | On Brand Color | Monochrome
```
- Proves versatility. Shows it works everywhere.

**In Context**
```
[2-3 photorealistic or styled mockups]
Business card | Website header | Social media | Signage
```
- Makes it REAL. This is what sells the concept.

### DECISION SLIDES

**Side by Side**
```
CONCEPT A          CONCEPT B          CONCEPT C
[Logo]             [Logo]             [Logo]
"The Anchor"       "The Shift"        "The Signal"
Bold & Grounded    Dynamic & Forward   Clean & Modern
```
- Clean comparison. Equal treatment. No bias (unless recommending).

**Recommendation (Optional)**
```
OUR RECOMMENDATION

We believe Concept [X] best serves [Brand] because:
- [Reason 1]
- [Reason 2]
- [Reason 3]
```
- Only include if the client wants your opinion.

**Next Steps**
```
WHAT'S NEXT

1. Select a direction
2. We refine and finalize
3. Deliver complete brand assets

[CTA: "Reply with your choice" or "Let's discuss"]
```

## PHASE 5 — VISUAL ELEVATION SUGGESTIONS

### How to Make It Premium

1. **Dark mode default** — Dark backgrounds (#0A0A0F) with white text feel more premium than white backgrounds
2. **One typeface, two weights** — Use a single premium sans-serif (Inter, Söhne, Aeonik) in Regular and Bold only
3. **Oversized numbers** — Section numbers at 200pt+ in 5% opacity create editorial depth
4. **Generous white space** — 40%+ of each slide should be empty
5. **No decorative elements** — No swooshes, gradients, or borders. The logo IS the design
6. **Consistent grid** — Every slide on the same 12-column grid
7. **Motion in presentation** — Subtle fade-in on reveal slides. The logo should "arrive" with presence.
8. **Paper-quality mockups** — Use photorealistic mockups with texture (not flat Canva-style)
9. **No more than 3 colors** in the presentation itself — Dark bg, white text, one accent (brand color)
10. **Print-ready PDF** — 1920×1080 landscape, 150+ DPI equivalent

### What to NEVER Include
- Clip art or stock icons ❌
- Gradients on text ❌
- More than 2 sentences per slide ❌
- Generic process diagrams ❌
- Watermarks ❌
- Too many font weights ❌
- Busy backgrounds ❌
